GTA Text — game kinh doanh doi thuc, chay 24/24

Day la mat nha cua mod "GTA Viet Life" tren GTA V. Game tren PC la ban day du; dien thoai la ban text de xem va giao dich khi Sep khong o may.

Cach chay 24/24

Toc do khi game tat60% so voi luc choi
1 ngay game= 48 phut thuc
Toi da bu mot lan14 ngay game
Khi game tat: quan van ban hang, tra luong, nop thue, tra tien mat bang, giao hang B2B, thu cong no. KHONG co doi/khat/met vi khong choi truc tiep. Trang nay hien so UOC TINH; so that chot luc mo game (game ap dung ca lenh tu dien thoai truoc).

Mo hinh kinh doanh

6 loai hinh: quan nuoc via he, tap hoa, F&B, FMCG, v.v. Moi loai co danh muc san pham rieng (20 mon), gia von, gia ban, tien nhap.
Kenh GT (truyen thong: via he, tap hoa, quan an): von nho, thue khoan co dinh moi ngay.
Kenh MT (hien dai: cua hang tien loi, sieu thi): chiet khau nhap hang, VAT/TNDN ~8% doanh thu.
B2C: ban le truc tiep cho khach tai quan.
B2B: 6 hop dong ban si (Co Lan, Ba Tu, Cay Me, Ngoc Lan, chuoi tien loi mini, Co.op Mart) — giao hang dinh ky, cong no phai thu theo ky han, co rui ro vo no.

Mat bang

4 hinh thuc: via he (phi ngay), ki-ot thue theo ngay, hop dong 30 ngay, mua dut. Cap quan toi da phu thuoc hinh thuc. Thieu tien thue 3 ngay lien tiep = bi thu hoi. Co cac cua hang that cua game goc (24/7, LTD, Los Santos Customs) lam mat bang (toa do gan dung).

Van hanh quan

Nhap hang theo lo 50 phan (mua 5 lo chiet khau 7%, 10 lo 12%). Thue toi da 4 nhan vien, luong $100/ngay moi nguoi. Nang cap cap quan ($2.500 x cap hien tai) de tang khach va gia ban. Het hang thi dung ban. Vung dong dan (trung tam, Vinewood) khach nhieu hon.

Giao dich tren dien thoai

Nhap 1/5/10 lo, thue / sa thai nhan vien, nang cap, rut quy quan. Lenh xep hang doi, game ap dung khi mo (hoac trong 10 giay neu dang choi). Thieu tien thi lenh that bai va hien o dau trang Kinh doanh.

Trong game (tren PC)

Noi chuyen bang giong voi NPC (giu N), NPC co tri nho. Nhu cau doi / khat / nang luong, nha thue vao duoc (phim E), noi that, blip tren ban do, giao hang lam them, phat khi vi pham, no co lai 5%/ngay, uy tin anh huong khach.
Ban do mo toan bo. Cua tren ban do la hinh khoi tinh nen khong mo duoc bang script.
